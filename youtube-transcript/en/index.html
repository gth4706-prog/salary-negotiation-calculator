Where do the captions come from

How it works

This pulls the captions YouTube already generated (auto or uploaded by the creator) and shows them with timestamps. Since it's not running new speech recognition, videos without captions aren't supported. Click a line and the video jumps to that moment.

Who this is for

Anyone who needs to find just one part of a long video, anyone turning a lecture into written notes, anyone repurposing a video's content into a blog post.

Not available right now

YouTube is currently blocking requests coming from data centers, so transcripts can't be fetched right now. Since requests go through a server, that's exactly where it's getting blocked, and we're looking for a workaround. We'll post an update once it's fixed.
