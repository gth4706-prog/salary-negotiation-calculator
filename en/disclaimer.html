1. What the results are

Our tools compute estimates from publicly available data and commonly accepted reference figures. No result carries legal or financial force, and none of it replaces professional judgment.

  • Salary and take-home pay figures apply Korea's published social insurance rates and simplified withholding tax table. Actual amounts vary with dependents, tax-free items, and company policy. This is not tax advice.
  • Severance and annual leave calculations follow general statutory rules; your employment contract, company rules, and individual circumstances can change the outcome. This is not labor law advice.
  • Percentile ("position") figures compare your input against an estimated baseline we set with reference to published wage statistics — not a full survey of actual salary distribution. Don't cite it as evidence in a negotiation.
  • Couple Referee is entertainment. It is not counseling or a legal determination, and no result should be taken as professional relationship advice.
  • Fishing tide and spot data is reference material built on public datasets. Tide forecasts can differ from reality with weather conditions, and coordinates carry error. Always verify conditions on site.

2. Safety notice

When using fishing information, check weather warnings and rough-sea advisories first, and wear a life jacket. Predicted and actual water levels can differ due to atmospheric pressure and wind. In Korea, report marine emergencies to the Coast Guard at 122.

If the Couple Referee detects signs of physical threat or violence, it stops ruling and shows support resources instead. If you are actually in danger, please contact 112 or Korea's Women's Emergency Hotline 1366.
