Contact

Bug reports · Feedback · Partnerships

If you've run into an issue, have thoughts on our calculation logic or data, or a partnership proposal, please reach out by email below. We usually reply within 2–3 days, though it may take a little longer when volume is high.

Email: contact@joeltool.com

What to include so we can help faster

For bug reports, three things are usually enough to reproduce the problem: which tool, what you entered, and what result you got. Telling us your browser (Chrome, Safari, etc.) and device (PC, iPhone, Android) helps too. Screenshots are welcome.

You don't need to send real salary figures or other sensitive numbers. An approximate range — "when I entered a base salary around 30 million KRW" — is plenty for us to track down the issue.

Frequently asked

My result differs from another site

Take-home pay depends on assumptions — number of dependents, tax-free items, which rate table is applied. This tool assumes a single-person household with a KRW 200,000 tax-free meal allowance by default. If another site defaults to two dependents or a different tax-free cap, the same salary can differ by tens of thousands of won per month. See the negotiation guide for details.

My role isn't in the list

We currently cover 22 representative roles and keep widening that set using Korea's official job classification. Let us know which role you'd like added by email and we'll consider prioritizing it.

Can I request a new tool?

Please do. Describe the specific situation — "I keep calculating this by hand and it's tedious" — and we'll look at whether it can become a tool. Several of the tools running here started from exactly that kind of request.

Can I quote or reuse data from this site?

You're free to use your own results however you like. Please don't clone the tools themselves or scrape the data in bulk for redistribution. If you quote us, a credit to joeltool.com is appreciated. For commercial use, please email us first.
