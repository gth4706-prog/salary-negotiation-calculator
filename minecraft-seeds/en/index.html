How are seeds picked

How the recommendation works

Answer four questions — edition, play style, the scenery you want to see, and what you'd like nearby — and each seed's description is analyzed and scored against your answers and sorted. A matching play style is worth 3 points, matching scenery 3 points, matching nearby structures 2 points, and seeds that match nothing are excluded from the candidates.

How to enter a seed

When creating a new world, paste the number straight into the seed field under More World Options. Java and Bedrock can generate different terrain from the same seed number, so check the edition shown on the card before you use it.

What this doesn't do

Seed data is compiled from public guide sites and communities — it's secondhand information that hasn't been re-verified in-game. Terrain may differ from the description. Versions vary too, so loading a seed on a different version than the one listed on the card will change the terrain. Note: seed descriptions themselves are shown in the original Korean, since the underlying dataset hasn't been translated.
