How do the 5 judges decide?

How it works

Each person writes their side and answers 6 multiple-choice questions. An AI reads that directly and rules from the perspective of 5 judges with different personalities. The Empathetic judge looks at who got hurt more; the Principled judge looks at whether a promise was broken and whether it's a repeat pattern. The Blunt judge only trusts what you admit to yourself, and the Reconciling judge cares less about fault than about what to do next. If there's no clear-cut fault, the judges won't force a side.

Why does each person write separately?

Because hearing only one side skews the ruling. What the first person writes stays hidden from the other, and when multiple-choice answers conflict, the tool trusts the side who admitted their own fault more.

What this doesn't do

This is for-fun reference content, not a legal or psychological judgment. Since it only works from what's written, any missing context isn't reflected. If there's physical threat or violence, it switches to support-resource guidance instead of ruling — that's not something to treat as fun. What you write is sent to an AI for judging and is not stored.
