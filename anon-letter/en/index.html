How does this letter keep going

How it works

When you arrive, you'll see one letter someone left. After reading it, write something for the next person, and it's saved as a reply (child) to that letter. The same letter can be shown to different people at different times, so a single letter can branch into several replies.

Why doesn't the chain ever break

If someone reads a letter and leaves without replying, that letter isn't lost — someone else can pick it up and continue it.

Can I write in a different language

Yes. Letters are stored in the language they were written in, and AI translates them on the fly for whoever's reading. A letter written in Korean can be read and continued by an English, Chinese, or Japanese speaker.

What this doesn't do

There's no account, so the server doesn't know who wrote what. Your browser remembers just the letters you personally wrote, so you can come back later and see "my branch" — if you clear your browser data, that record is gone.
