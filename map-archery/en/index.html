🏹 Can't pick a destination? Shoot for it

The map keeps spinning. Pull back the bowstring to aim, then let go — the arrow flies. Wherever it lands the instant it hits, that district becomes your trip. Since the map never stops turning, the same aim never gives the same result twice.

ℹ️ Where does this map data come from?
District boundaries from Statistics Korea's SGIS service, released under Korea Open Government License Type 1, via an open-source processed dataset — simplified further for this tool. All 250 districts nationwide are included; the coordinates shown are each district's approximate center point.

How this tool works

Controls

Press anywhere on the canvas and drag away from your target — like drawing a bow — and hold. The angle you pull sets the direction, the distance sets the power. Release, and the arrow flies. Where it lands depends on both your aim and exactly where the map happened to be spinning at that instant.

How many times can I shoot?

Unlimited. Keep shooting until you get a destination you like. If the arrow flies past the map into open water, just shoot again.

What this doesn't do

It won't tell you where to eat, stay, or what to see — just the district name and its rough coordinates. Use it as a starting point for picking a trip, not a full guide. It doesn't go down to the neighborhood (eup/myeon/dong) level.
