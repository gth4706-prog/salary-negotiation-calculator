Bed Sizes Explained — Twin, Full, Queen, King

Picking the right bed size for a studio or small room

Published 2026-08-01 · Updated 2026-08-03

A bed takes up more floor space than any other piece of furniture in a room, so getting the size wrong can leave no room for anything else. Here are the standard dimensions (width × length, in mm) used in Korea, which map closely to Twin/Full/Queen/King sizing elsewhere.

SizeWidth × LengthRecommended room size
Twin (S)1000×2000mmStudio, 3 pyeong or less (~10m²)
Full / Super Single (SS)1100×2000mmStudio, 4–5 pyeong (~13–16m²)
Queen (Q)1500×2000mm6+ pyeong (~20m²), fits two people
King (K)1600×2000mm8+ pyeong (~26m²), larger rooms

Full/Super Single is the safe default for a studio

For a single-person household, Full/Super Single is the most commonly chosen size. It sleeps more comfortably than a Twin without eating up as much floor space as a Queen, leaving room for a desk or storage.

Before you pick a size

  • Leave at least 50–60cm along the side of the bed so you can make it or vacuum comfortably.
  • Factor in mattress thickness (typically 20–25cm) — the actual bed height ends up higher than expected.
  • Check that the bed's position doesn't block the door's swing.
